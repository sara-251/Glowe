Our story

Glowé started with a simple belief,skincare should be honest,effective
and kind to your skin and the planet.

How it began

Glowé was founded in 2020 by a small team of skincare enthusiasts who were tired of long ingredient lists full of things they couldn't pronounce. We wanted skincare that worked, felt good and didn't cost the earth.

Our mission

To make effective, clean skincare accessible to everyone. We formulate every product with dermatologist tested, cruelty free ingredients, and we're honest about what goes in the bottle and why.
